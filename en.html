Salon Policies

To ensure a relaxing and punctual experience for every client, please review our guidelines prior to your visit.

01

CANCELLATIONS & NO-SHOWS

  • Rescheduling / Cancellation: Free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment.
  • Late Cancellation (< 24h): A fee equivalent to 40% of the booked services will apply.
  • No-Show: In case of a missed appointment without notice, 100% of the service cost will be charged prior to booking any future appointment.
  • Credit Card: A valid credit card is required on file when booking online to secure your appointment.
02

LATE ARRIVALS & PUNCTUALITY

  • Notice: Please contact us by phone (438-777-7043) or email if you expect to be late.
  • 15-Minute Grace Period: After 15 minutes without notice, the appointment will be considered a no-show and your spot may be offered to another client.
  • Service Adjustment: In case of an accepted late arrival, your service may be shortened to respect the next client's schedule at the original full price.
03

COMFORT & GUESTS

  • Arrive Alone: To maintain a calm and safe atmosphere, please avoid bringing extra guests (including children or pets) unless agreed upon in advance.
  • Client Profile: Complete contact information (name, phone, email) is required upon registration for appointment tracking.
04

PRICING & PROMOTIONS

  • Price Review: All prices are subject to change without notice.
  • Promotions: Promotions and discounts cannot be combined. Rescheduling a promotional appointment outside the valid period results in losing the discounted rate.
  • Senior Technicians: Services performed by our senior specialists are excluded from promotional offers unless specified otherwise.
05

SERVICE GUARANTEE & PRODUCT RETURNS

  • Services (7-Day Guarantee): No monetary refunds are issued for rendered services. If you experience any issues with your set or polish, contact us within 7 days of your visit: we will gladly perform a touch-up.
  • Products: Opened or used items are final sale and non-refundable. Only defective or damaged products upon purchase may be exchanged or converted into salon credit.

Thank you for your trust and understanding. — The Tui - Nail Salon & Spa Team

Careers at Tui

Join a passionate and caring team in the heart of Montreal. We are looking for creative and meticulous nail artists.

01

OPEN POSITIONS

  • Nail Technician: Mastery in Shellac, UV gel, Gel-X, BIAB, or dip powder.
  • Manicure & Pedicure Specialist: Complete aesthetic care, regular/gel polish, and spa treatments.
  • Flexible Hours: Full-time and part-time positions available based on your schedule.
02

WHY CHOOSE TUI NAIL SPA?

  • Competitive Compensation: Attractive salary + generous commissions + 100% of tips.
  • Established Loyal Clientele: Salon located at 1570 Rue Saint-Timothée (Ville-Marie) with a steady flow of appointments.
  • High-End Environment: Modern equipment, strict hygiene standards, and a warm atmosphere.
03

PROFILE SOUGHT

  • Craftsmanship: Attention to detail, impeccable cleanliness, and a passion for nail aesthetics.
  • Customer Service: Welcoming attitude, punctuality, and team spirit.
  • Languages: Functional French and/or English (an appreciated asset).
04

HOW TO APPLY?

  • Online (Quick): Apply in 2 minutes via our StaffedUp portal.
  • By Email: Send your resume or portfolio photos to tuinailspa@gmail.com.
  • By Phone: Contact us directly at 438-777-7043 to schedule an interview.
