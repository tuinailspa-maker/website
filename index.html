Salon Policies

To ensure a relaxing and punctual experience for every client, please review our salon guidelines.

01

CANCELLATIONS & NO-SHOWS

  • Rescheduling / Cancellation: Free up to 24 hours before your appointment.
  • Late Cancellation (< 24h): 40% of booked services.
  • No-Show: 100% of booked service cost charged before any new booking.
  • Credit Card: Required on file during online booking.
02

LATE ARRIVALS & PUNCTUALITY

  • Notice: Contact us by phone (438-777-7043).
  • 15-Minute Grace Period: After 15 minutes, the appointment is considered a no-show.
  • Service Adjustment: Your service may be shortened to respect the next client's schedule.
03

COMFORT & GUESTS

  • Come Alone: To maintain a calm atmosphere, please avoid bringing guests.
  • Client Record: Complete contact info required for tracking.
04

PRICING & PROMOTIONS

  • Price Revision: Subject to change without notice.
  • Special Offers: Cannot be combined.
05

SERVICE GUARANTEE & RETURNS

  • Services (7-Day Guarantee): No monetary refunds. If issues arise within 7 days, free touch-up provided.
  • Products: Final sale, except for defective items upon purchase.
